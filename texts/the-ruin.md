# The Ruin

Preserved in the Exeter Book, 49 lines, probably 8th-9th century. An elegy on
a ruined Roman city, often identified with Bath. A large diagonal burn across
the manuscript page has destroyed parts of the text; those gaps are marked
`[...]` below, matching the damage in the original — not a transcription
error. Old English text: Wikipedia, "The Ruin" (CC BY-SA 4.0), reflecting
standard scholarly editions of the Exeter Book. Modern English translation:
own work for this repository, prose rendering (not verse); untranslated
gaps mark manuscript damage, not omitted content.

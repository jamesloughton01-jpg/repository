# Maxims I

204 lines, preserved in the Exeter Book (in three loosely-linked sections,
often called A, B, and C). A collection of gnomic wisdom-sayings in the
characteristic Old English "sceal" (shall/must) formula, describing the
fitting, proper nature of people, animals, and things — a genre related to
the Old Norse *Hávamál*. Old English text: sacred-texts.com, Cook &
Tinker, *Select Translations from Old English Poetry* (1902), public
domain. Modern English translation: own work for this repository, prose
rendering (not verse).

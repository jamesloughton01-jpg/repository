# The Capture of the Five Boroughs

13 lines, embedded in the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle (Parker manuscript, "A")
under the year 942 AD. One of five short "Chronicle poems" scattered
through the Chronicle's annals, alongside The Battle of Brunanburh (937,
also in this collection) and two later poems on King Edgar. Celebrates
King Edmund I's reconquest of the "Five Boroughs" of the East Midlands
— Leicester, Lincoln, Nottingham, Stamford, and Derby — fortified towns
of the Danelaw that had fallen under Norse rule; Edmund brought them back
under English control and freed their (Christianized, English-speaking)
Danish inhabitants from Norse overlordship. Old English text: John Earle
(ed.), *Two of the Saxon Chronicles Parallel* (Oxford: Clarendon Press,
1892), public domain (Earle died in 1903; the edition is over 130 years
old). Modern English translation: own work for this repository, prose
rendering (not verse).

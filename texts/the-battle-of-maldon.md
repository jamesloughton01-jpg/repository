# The Battle of Maldon

Commemorates the real Battle of Maldon (991 AD). Preserved in a single
manuscript (Cotton Otho A.xii) destroyed in the 1731 Cotton library fire;
survives only because Thomas Hearne copied and published it in 1726. Both
the beginning and ending of the poem are lost — it opens and closes
mid-narrative. Old English text: *Bright's Anglo-Saxon Reader* (James W.
Bright), via Wikisource, CC BY-SA (underlying poem is public domain, author
unknown). Modern English translation: own work for this repository, prose
rendering (not verse). Line numbering here starts at 1 for the surviving
fragment's first line, not standard manuscript numbering.

# Cædmon's Hymn

West Saxon version. The oldest datable Old English poem (composed c. 658-680),
recorded by Bede in his *Ecclesiastical History*. Old English text: sacred-texts.com,
Cook & Tinker, *Select Translations from Old English Poetry* (1902), public domain.
Modern English translation: own work for this repository.

| # | Old English | Modern English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nu sculon herigean heofonrices weard, | Now we must praise the Guardian of the heavenly kingdom, |
| 2 | meotodes meahte and his modgeþanc, | the might of the Measurer and the thought of his mind, |
| 3 | weorc wuldorfæder, swa he wundra gehwæs, | the work of the Father of glory, as he, of every wonder, |
| 4 | ece drihten, or onstealde. | the eternal Lord, established the beginning. |
| 5 | He ærest sceop eorðan bearnum | He first created, for the children of earth, |
| 6 | heofon to hrofe, halig scyppend; | heaven as a roof, the holy Creator; |
| 7 | þa middangeard moncynnes weard | then mankind's guardian made middle-earth, |
| 8 | ece drihten, æfter teode | the eternal Lord, afterward, |
| 9 | firum foldan, frea ælmihtig. | the earth for men, the Lord almighty. |
